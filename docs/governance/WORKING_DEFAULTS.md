# Quirk working defaults

Basis: Bryan's direction of 2026-10-14 to persist the Capability Watch recommendations and consider hierarchy throughout Quirk work. This document records that working direction; repository admission, canonical definitions, runtime authorization, and deployment retain their existing owners and gates. It adds no concept IDs or execution grants.

## Apply according to scope

Preserve Quirk as a creative medium, capability ecosystem, and participatory world. Apply these defaults across design, building, mapping, implementation, code, creation, prompting, execution, research, learning loops, graphs, worldbuilding, extensions, events, tools, skills, plugins, resources, documentation, plans, notes, and changelogs. A tiny task needs only the relevant part.

Current explicit instructions and applicable access constraints govern the task. Defaults remain revisable and cannot override higher-priority instructions or another person's choices. Use existing accepted contracts and sources. Do not turn a recommendation, retrieved source, successful check, or installed tool into authority.

## Hierarchy and relationships

| Layer | Owns | Child behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared Quirk principles | Breadth, protected distinctions, shared working defaults | Inherit applicable principles without redefining canon |
| Domain | Domain meaning, methods, contracts, taste scope | Extend within the domain owner's remit |
| Project or system | Intended outcome, architecture, resources, accepted decisions | Declare parent, dependencies, local constraints |
| Component or capability | One responsibility, inputs, outputs, effects, interface | Inherit project guidance; state meaningful differences only |
| Task or run | Present intent, actor, scope, budget, acceptance evidence | Select relevant context; keep the run's basis traceable |
| Session and artifact | Work state, exact sources, results, unresolved next step | Checkpoint and link corrections; preserve historical originals |

Hierarchy organizes responsibility and context; it does not grant access. Keep containment/inheritance separate from dependency, provenance, temporal order, extension, and authority relationships. Use the owning model's typed relations; do not create a new taxonomy merely to draw a graph. Allow multiple dependencies or interpretations without inventing multiple authoritative parents.

For substantial work, identify the owning source, parent, affected consumers, interfaces, and lifecycle. For local differences, record scope, reason, owner, source revision, and reopening condition when relevant. Resolve conflicts with applicable instructions and the actual source owner; recency or hierarchy depth alone does not win. Pause only the affected action when a required conflict cannot be resolved.

## Prioritize useful work

Use dependency and consequence before novelty or tool count. The Capability Watch recommendation order is: comparable observations and prior-proposal reconciliation; one coherent integration proof and a bounded command/effect map where needed; one useful capability trial; broader runtime enforcement or market exploration when the chosen work needs it. This is a sequencing default, not a requirement to implement the whole roadmap.

Explain each recommendation through its evidence, expected benefit, uncertainty, prerequisites, responsible role, required skills/resources, smallest method, completion evidence, and stop or rollback condition. Distinguish a proposed owner from an accepted assignment. Give resource estimates in useful units and label them as estimates; protect human attention alongside time, compute, money, and maintenance.

Use the existing Work Queue and source artifacts. Add a record only when no existing owner can hold the state. Prefer one bounded outcome, a small work-in-progress limit, and the cheapest observation that could change the decision. Compare against the ordinary workflow when judging improvement. Stop optional tests and analysis once the material uncertainty and required gates are resolved.

## Evidence and learning

Preserve raw sources, original expression, exact revisions when available, and transformation lineage. Separate observation, inference, proposal, human preference, process disposition, and outcome. Repeated summaries of one source are one lineage. Unknown, inaccessible, incomplete, untried, deferred, declined, failed, and successful are distinct states; silence is not approval or evidence of value.

For change detection, compare stable identities within equivalent source, host, account/access, filtering, pagination, and observation contexts. Retain raw and normalized captures with parser version and coverage. Missing or truncated data cannot prove removal. Fingerprint package version, callable surface/schema, descriptions, and effects separately. An unchanged schema cannot guarantee unchanged remote behavior.

Describe effects concretely: reads, writes, spending, data disclosure, metadata changes, and external consequences. Reuse compatible existing contracts and receipts rather than minting substitutes. Test the disputed behavior, including relevant error, retry, replay, and recovery cases; structural validation proves only what it checked.

A learning loop needs an observation, bounded hypothesis, baseline, method, measured result, disposition, and wanted reopening condition. Keep process closure separate from evidence: retiring a proposal can leave its outcome UNKNOWN. Preserve alternatives and deliberate creative departures. Human benefit and taste require actual human evidence; quiet participation and private unfinished work remain valid.

## Continuity and propagation

Maintain one owning source and small applicable projections. Child assets inherit the parent profile; duplicate a full policy only when an offline consumer needs a versioned export. Keep historical work pinned to its original basis and link corrections or successors.

At creation, resume, and material change, read the applicable current source, select a coherent revision, inspect local differences and affected dependencies, then update only the affected consumers. Preserve concurrent edits. Record source revision, meaningful change, delivered target, verification, limits, and recovery in the existing artifact or changelog.

Track authored, persisted, delivered, loaded, behavior-tested, admitted, and deployed separately. A save proves persistence; a prompt update proves configuration; a draft PR proves a reviewable proposal. None establishes universal adoption. Report stale, incompatible, unreachable, or unobserved consumers honestly.

Scheduled reviews retain separate last-attempt and last-successful state, covered period, source references, coverage, findings, prior-proposal outcomes, and deduplication keys. Retry only on relevant change or an actually missed/failed intended period. An unchanged blocker does not justify repeated alerts. If state cannot be saved, report that limit and do not claim the checkpoint advanced.

## Change discipline

Preserve authorization already provided for relevant reversible work. Do not introduce repeated confirmation for the same scope, and do not expand to unrequested publication, spending, external messaging, protected runtime changes, or canonical promotion.

Use the owning repository's validation and receipt rules. Corrections to these defaults update the source and affected projections, with exact scope and evidence. New systems, tools, skills, plugins, and documents must identify their owner and inherited guidance; they do not each need a new governance system.

## Power, people, creativity, and portability

Applied direction: Bryan's 2026-10-14 approval of the nine top-level changes. These extend the existing defaults, not a new constitution, universal checklist, quality score, or execution grant. Select only the distinctions needed for the present work. Installed originals and historical trials keep their own identities and evidence.

### 1. Useful work before machinery

Begin with a person's intended result. REUSE, COMPOSE, or EXTEND an existing procedure before building another when it serves that result. A new name alone does not justify a skill, service, repository, or required document. Separation needs a distinct responsibility, interface, or failure boundary. Expression, pleasure, surprise, belonging, and learning can justify work; immediate revenue and efficiency are not universal tests. Active machinery must earn its upkeep; dormant creative material need not.

### 2. Shared exchange, varied thinking

Keep identity, source revision, protected choices, uncertainty, and permission boundaries in small shared references. Require only the fields relevant to the exchange. Keep stable technical IDs with expressive names and aliases; plain language must remain usable. Preserve original material alongside interpretations. A lyric, sketch, incident, and research question need not follow the same document sequence. Do not reinterpret an old identifier or replace an original with its summary.

### 3. Bound consequences, not exploration

Match checks to concrete effects. Continue already-authorized reversible work without repeated confirmation. Recheck the affected boundary when resources, audience, spending, permissions, or effects change. Stop only the dependent action when possible; an unavailable publisher need not stop private drafting. Host-enforced permissions remain necessary: instructions and favorable evaluations cannot grant access. A fictional game unlock, score, character role, or simulation success never authorizes real publication, payment, credentials, or delegation.

### 4. Participant agency

Scope preferences and contributions to their actual person, purpose, and audience. Preserve disagreement and the right to decline or leave. Provide understandable inspection, correction, export, preference bypass, and withdrawal paths where personal records are used; propagate withdrawals to derived views. Record credit and permitted reuse. Do not make one person's taste a universal default. Delegate only to the appropriate decision owner, not automatically to the founder. Do not claim these rights are implemented merely because they are documented. Retention and deletion must not preserve personal payloads under an indiscriminate immutable-history rule.

### 5. Factors do not define possibility

Keep hard constraints, contextual preferences, and testable hypotheses separate. Permission is not a weighted quality factor. Allow neither, different purpose, and cannot judge yet; keep deliberate departures from past taste available. Preserve the reason and context of a preference, including choices made to protect a later reveal. Use task-specific comparisons instead of a universal Quirk quality score. Record sensitivity or unresolved trade-offs rather than invent precision. Exploration budgets govern effort, not artistic worth.

### 6. Prove concrete portability

Test a named operation between named execution surfaces at recorded revisions: access, meaning preservation, continuation, return, and permission preservation are separate observations. Format validity is not a completed handoff. An attachment route is distinct from shared-storage synchronization. Keep existing Roundtrip 01 and fresh-reader baselines frozen; a new synthetic route cannot fill their missing evidence. Use existing standards and adapters where they fit without assuming universal host support.

### 7. Authority belongs to the record, not the platform

Identify the governing source and accepted writer or reconciliation rule for each mutable record. Custody, search, dashboards, summaries, and database projections do not acquire semantic authority by storing it. Preserve source identity and base revision through adapters. Surface stale or competing revisions; never silently overwrite newer work. Reuse existing record and proposal stores rather than create a parallel registry. Export must preserve meaning and declare losses; it does not expand the recipient's access.

### 8. Outcomes and burden are separate evidence

Compare a proposed improvement with the ordinary workflow using equivalent inputs and stated limits. Record protected-choice preservation, task completion, correction, review, recovery, and maintenance burden where actually observed. Keep estimates, structural checks, deterministic behavior, fresh-agent behavior, human preference, and authorization distinct. Unknown effort is not zero; a replay is not another independent participant. Do not choose a winner for a human or count an unrun trial as a success. Retire optional active machinery that does not justify its burden without deleting useful source material.

### 9. Recoverable commitments

Choose a useful current path without promising compatibility with everything. Keep exact sources, dependencies, cancellation, safe resume, and a practicable export/manual path. For effectful operations, bind retry identity to the intended authorized operation and reconcile uncertain partial completion before retrying; a timeout does not prove no effect occurred. Demonstrate failure and recovery before making a component a consequential dependency. A rollback does not erase consequences or prove nobody was affected.

### Apply through existing companions

Quirk Plugin Planner uses its existing REUSE/COMPOSE/EXTEND/BUILD/HOLD/DROP decisions and keeps N1/N2 local alternatives as trials until compared. Game Studio reuses an existing game or fixture before selecting another engine; playable fiction and real tool authority stay separate. Superpowers supplies scoped execution and verification, not extra approvals for already-authorized reversible work. Supabase candidate rows and Resource Inbox metadata remain projections, not activation. One shared source and one tested consumer are preferable to copies across every plugin.

For several recommendations, provide stable short selection labels when useful and accept mixed dispositions in one reply. A selection may mean apply, challenge, revise, or park; do not silently convert all selections into adoption. Historical A/C working principles, challengeable B, and parked D retain their recorded scope until explicitly changed.
