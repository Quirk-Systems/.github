# Quirk Systems

**Turn human expression into things people can explore, make, test, and
improve—while keeping the meaning, evidence, and human choice in view.**

Quirk is an evolving collection of creative work, experiments, software, and
shared practices. Its ambition is to connect conversations, capabilities,
evidence, decisions, and experiences in ways that remain inspectable and
useful. Pieces are at different stages. A repository, plan, or appealing name
does not by itself establish a released product or an integrated system.

## Find a starting point

| If you are here to… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| Explore an experience or play | Look at a project's README for what can actually be tried and how to get access. If the experience or invitation is unclear, ask through [support](https://github.com/Quirk-Systems/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md). |
| Design, create, make, or tell a story | Share a bounded idea or example through the relevant project's issue or contribution route. Keep the original, its context, and intended audience visible. |
| Build, develop, or contribute | Choose a repository below, read its README and local instructions, and follow the [contributor guide](https://github.com/Quirk-Systems/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). |
| Operate, review, or make a decision | Start with the affected repository's operating and evidence guidance. Confirm the current target, permissions, and observed state before taking action. |
| Report a barrier or problem | Use the relevant repository's issue route or [support guidance](https://github.com/Quirk-Systems/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md). Report security concerns privately as described in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Quirk-Systems/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). |

A player, game master, designer, marketer, maker, new user, or experienced
contributor may take more than one path. A title does not grant access or
authority over someone else's choices.

## How we work

- Preserve human intent and meaningful originals when turning an idea into a
  brief, experiment, artifact, or implementation.
- Distinguish evidence, interpretation, a candidate, an accepted decision, and
  a separately authorized effect. A green check proves only what it checked.
- Give agents and automation a bounded task and appropriate permissions;
  human authority remains specific to the action and context.
- Keep versioned definitions, runtime behavior, and data projections distinct.
  A copy or database row does not silently become the owner of a definition.
- Create a new repository when a real boundary justifies it, such as a second
  consumer, security, ownership, independent release, or deployment need.
- Learn from outcomes and corrections. A user's choice in one experience is
  not automatically a permanent preference or personality claim.
